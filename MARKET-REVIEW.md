Implemented: whole-project positioning, a custom color concept explorer, palette handoff into the estimate request, multi-service inquiry option, and a custom-design FAQ. Existing actual project photos, customer reviews and video remain in place.

Do not claim unrelated building trades, certifications, warranties, turnaround times, or search rankings without evidence. Specialty finish availability is confirmed during scope review. Color concepts are illustrations, not actual projects or exact paint matches. The request form prepares an SMS; the customer still needs to send it.
