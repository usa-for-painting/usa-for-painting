Where do you work?+

We serve residential and commercial customers in New Castle County, Delaware, including the Newark area. Tell us your project’s location and we’ll confirm availability.

Is the estimate free?+

Yes. Contact us to discuss your project and request a free estimate. The scope, surface condition, preparation, and finish will help us work out your project’s pricing.

Do you paint both interiors and exteriors?+

Yes. We provide interior and exterior painting for homes and commercial properties. We also welcome inquiries about cabinet painting, trim, and specialty painting.

How long will my project take?+

Timing depends on the size of the space, preparation, drying time, and weather for exterior work. We’ll discuss the expected schedule with you before the project begins.

What should I prepare before painting?+

We’ll agree on the preparation plan with you. This may include moving small or valuable items and arranging access to the work area. We’ll discuss furniture protection and surface preparation as part of the scope.
