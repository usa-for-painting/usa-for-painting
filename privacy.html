Your privacy matters.

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Information you choose to share

The estimate form creates a text message on your device. Entering information into the form does not send it to us. You decide whether to send the prepared message through your messaging app. Please avoid including sensitive information in your project description.

If you call or text us, we receive the contact information and project details you share and use them to respond to your inquiry, prepare an estimate, or coordinate your project.

Website services

This website is designed for hosting on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as your IP address, to deliver and secure the website. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives connection information when those fonts are requested. Optional Google Analytics is enabled only when configured and only after you choose Allow analytics. It measures page visits, engagement, video viewing, and clicks on project or contact links. It does not receive the names, phone numbers, postal codes, or messages you type into the estimate form. Advertising features are disabled. You may change your choice through Analytics preferences in the footer when analytics is available.

Your device

Form details remain on the current page until you leave or reload it. The Copy message button writes the prepared message to your clipboard only when you choose that action. Your messaging provider handles messages you send according to its own policies.

Questions or requests

For questions about information you have shared with USA For Painting, please call (302) 452-4001.
